MISSION ACHIEVEMENTS · 系统能力

◈ AUTONOMOUS DATA COLLECTION 24/7 unattended sampling at the Antarctic field site. 在南极现场无人值守，全天候自动采样。
▣ MULTI-SENSOR INTEGRATION Temperature, humidity, solar, power and GPS channels in one node. 单节点集成温度、湿度、太阳辐射、电源与 GPS 等多类传感器。
⚡ LOW-POWER OPERATION Solar powered with battery buffering for long-term polar operation. 太阳能供电加电池缓冲，支持极地长期低功耗运行。
⌁ LONG-RANGE LoRa COMMUNICATION Low-power long-range radio uplink for remote data retrieval. 低功耗远距离 LoRa 无线链路，远程回传数据。
✛ GPS POSITIONING Autonomous satellite positioning for deployment tracking. 自主卫星定位，记录部署位置与运行轨迹。
